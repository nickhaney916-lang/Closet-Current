Garment and shadow outline draft

Internal benchmark tool. Trace true clothing and attached cast shadows as separate polygons. This is not a customer-facing step. Drafts require independent review before use as ground truth.

Load an original image, choose its ID, then tap around the garment or shadow. Finish each polygon with at least three points.

Export is a draft only. Matching SHA-256 is required for later validation; the file is not automatically uploaded.
