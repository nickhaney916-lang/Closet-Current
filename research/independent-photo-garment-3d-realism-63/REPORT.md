## P63 change

P62's source-mask call supplied literal reviewConfirmed=true and native admission did not require a matching owner review receipt. A correct SHA proves source bytes; it does not attest a person reviewed the cutout. P63 removes that implicit approval and requires an owner-bound receipt matching the owner, saved garment ID, original photo SHA, cutout SHA, approved status, and review ID at both geometry measurement and native admission. The present pinned blouse fixture provides no such receipt, so its 3D path remains unavailable and the user retains the original photo fallback.

Synthetic test receipts only exercise receipt matching and rejection. They are not real approvals and do not change the fixture's REVIEW status.
