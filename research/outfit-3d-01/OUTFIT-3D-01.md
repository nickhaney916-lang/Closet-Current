## Audited gaps in existing source
Existing 3D68 source index verifies authenticated owner and source-original JPEG SHA, but is read-only and refuses an entire snapshot on one unsupported item. Existing public/outfit-board.js stores actual owner outfits in the state objectStore under the outfit-board key. Its current cleanOutfitBoard normalizer silently drops deleted referenced IDs and all outerwear when a dress is selected. Both require follow-up production integration to preserve missing references and source-verified dress plus jacket (as an independent 2D reference until its real mesh is approved).

The available source-approved 3D system handles a **specific** original Commons photographed shirt with two specific genuine denim originals, plus a specific original photographed dress. It does NOT certify a newly photographed blouse, skirt, shoes, outer jacket, shorts, or arbitrary camera image as an independently reconstructed 3D mesh. Any missing certified 3D input stays in original-photograph or 2D fallback; unphotographed sides and rear are neutral representation, never presented as original photographic evidence.
