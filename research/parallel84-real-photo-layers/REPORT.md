## Verified underlying engineering gap

C52's original 3,836-triangle licensed Commons-0053 dress scan measures source-vs-source surfaces of **one** garment. It does not certify the real inter-garment intersections of a worn shirt+jeans, dress+jacket, top+outer layer, or shoes, nor natural cloth behavior. Existing P110 public research look metadata pins shirt+two jeans source hashes, but source identity metadata alone cannot establish two genuine photographed garments simultaneously fitted, human-approved and rendered.
