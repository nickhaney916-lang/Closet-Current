## Specific defect confirmed against genuine source-locked model proposals

Earlier [PR #575](https://github.com/nickhaney916-lang/Closet-Current/pull/575) genuine SegFormer benchmark recorded **19 original licensed JPEG photos / 35 single-reviewer visible clothing annotations**. In its `commons-0021` public-domain *Jeans for men* photograph, the model produced a shirt region and jeans region correctly matched and two **separate** shoe fragments, neither wide enough to match the human annotated shoe pair. Existing `visibleGarmentGroups24` shoe-pair heuristic rejects them because horizontal gap is **16.13% of photo width**, just outside its old 15% rule; shoe heights overlap by ~76% of the shorter height and their widths are comparable. Combining the two historical crop bounding boxes produces **0.921 geometric IoU** with the existing shoe-pair annotated rectangle. This is a retrospective observation, not independently certified item identity.

## Surgical code adjustment

`research/multi-garment-capture-01/shoe-pair-geometry-30.mjs` adds a pure guarded same-level shoe-pair eligibility and normalized/pixel bounding union. Preserves prior 15%/12%-vertical legacy pairing; only extends horizontally to at most **22% of photo width** AND **1.8× the narrower shoe width** when the two regions have vertical center difference ≤8.5% of height, at least 50% shared vertical overlap of shorter shoe height, and width ratio ≤2.5. Invalid bounding boxes reject. The actual model-dependent `research/multi-garment-capture-01/visible-groups.mjs` uses this pure helper to suggest a **single unverified shoe-pair review crop**, with original source-mask pixel membership, instance keys, original garment photo source and unchanged color samples/recognition. Clothing type, left vs right side, whether both shoes are same physical pair and 3D readiness remain **UNVERIFIED**. No automatic save; private closet continues existing user confirmation and owner/source-SHA atomic protections.

Uses the new pure geometry criterion to perform a *BOX UNION PROXY* only on the recorded original shoe-region bounding boxes. Expected score, in EACH browser: original **10/35 annotated garments matched among 61 proposals** (51 unmatched); proxy **11/35 matched among 60 proposals** (49 unmatched), with only `commons-0021` affected and no other source output changed. Do **NOT** claim these new scores are from re-running SegFormer or the true joint raw pixel mask — the archived model artifact includes output crop rectangles but not per-pixel logits/masks. This historical cohort was inspected before setting thresholds, so the 11/35 is **post-hoc, not a new unseen holdout or evidence of generalization**. Even if this small targeted gain is confirmed, overall instance detection remains very poor, and cropped/overlapping/flat-lay same-class garments still require major model work.
