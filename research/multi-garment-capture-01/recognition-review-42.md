The opt-in button runs the existing local FashionCLIP worker on the verified source then on the reviewed crop. The pinned vision model is `ff13/fashion-clip@0f99955d944c39bfd9d035a02ac273c7a44a406b`, ONNX q8 WASM with Transformers.js 3.8.1. The original result is context only. The normalized crop suggestion has no authority to edit fields or approve a save. Unknown labels are explicitly displayed as uncertain. Saved review evidence is separate from user-selected fields.

The adapter invalidates its request on recrop, removal, new source, reset, switching the target garment or beginning Save. Completion also checks the current owner, source epoch and piece identity. The immutable snapshot and existing atomic private save retain the JPEG and distinct source-region keys.
