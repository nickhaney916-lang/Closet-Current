# Offline avatar flow — standalone synthetic proof of interaction Open this file locally in a browser. No network, account access, photo upload, paid AI, or app integration. The two clothing tiles are **synthetic SVG fixtures**, not extracted fabric from real photographs. Click a garment to animate its transfer to the figure, switch items, or choose a dress. A user-facing production pipeline would still need: original-image garment masks, texture-preserving UV mapping or image warping, occlusion handling, pose-aware fit rendering, and independent human-reviewed garment-fidelity tests.
