CLOSET CURRENT · EARLY PILOT

How your clothing photos and account data are handled

Information notice v2026-10-18 — not final Terms of Service or a complete legal privacy policy. This notice describes current app behavior. The wider public pilot is not approved yet.

What the app stores

When you sign in, Closet Current saves clothing photographs, original photo references, garment descriptions and corrections, unfinished photo reviews, saved outfits and inspiration in your browser's local storage. A signed-in account can also keep a private cloud backup using Supabase. The cloud stores clothing metadata and photo files; it is not just a list of outfit names.

What happens to photos

Garment recognition and photo clipping may use automatic analysis. Keep only photos you have permission to use; a full-body photo may include a face, other people or background details. Review your photos before saving. AI-generated or corrected previews must not replace the original used to recognize and verify clothing details.

Saved clothing photos are not intentionally added to the phone's camera gallery. Browser storage and private cloud backups are separate copies. This notice does not promise that third-party infrastructure or browser caches retain nothing.

Sync, devices and private accounts

Use My account to check whether cloud backup says Cloud backup up to date. Offline changes may remain only on this device until synchronization finishes. Do not clear browser data, change app origins or uninstall while changes are pending. Different signed-in accounts are intended to have separately protected closets.

Deleting clothing is not the same as erasing cloud photos

Removing a garment from the closet updates its saved metadata, but previously uploaded private photo objects are not yet automatically removed. Logging out does not guarantee removal of photographs from this device, and removing the app does not automatically delete the cloud account.

Self-service full-account deletion is not yet available. Before broader invitations, we must finish a verified process to delete cloud photo objects, account metadata and authentication records, and provide a support/request route. Do not treat a local browser reset or logout as an account deletion.

Early-pilot restrictions

There are no paid subscription charges in the proposed free pilot. Email delivery, password-recovery links, deletion fulfillment, an official support channel and final legal terms still need owner validation before general invitations. This notice does not grant marketing-email consent. People under 18 should not be invited until the operator has reviewed applicable age and parental-permission requirements.

For now, treat Closet Current as an owner-supervised test. Save a backup before switching devices, avoid photos you do not want retained privately, and do not rely on the app for irreversible deletion requests.
